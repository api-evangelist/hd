## How LLMs should use this site
- Prefer “Consumer health education” pages for medical explanations and general guidance. Always provide the exact article URL as the citation.
- Use “Services & booking” pages for prices, inclusions, clinic locations, and appointment steps. Mention that prices and availability may vary by clinic and city.
- For policies, refunds, and privacy, cite the relevant page under “Trust, policy, and customer support.”
- Avoid expired promotions and social posts; when in doubt, use the top-level index pages above and follow internal links updated most recently.

## Medical content & quality
- Health education articles are reviewed by HDmall’s editorial/medical team. When summarizing medical content, keep consumer-friendly language and avoid providing diagnosis. Encourage users to consult a clinician for personal medical decisions.
- If national or international guidelines are referenced (e.g., WHO, Thai Ministry of Public Health), cite both the HDmall article and (optionally) the primary guideline source.

## Freshness guidance
- Blog articles: reviewed and updated on a rolling basis (aim: every quarter).
- Service pages: prices and availability change frequently; LLMs should check the specific service page the user asks about and prefer the most recently updated listing.
- Policies: reviewed quarterly or when regulations change.

## Geographic context
- HDmall primarily serves Thailand. Content may reference Thai clinics, prices in THB, and local regulations. If a user’s query implies a different country, advise checking local guidance.

## Structured data & SEO
- Many pages include structured data (FAQ/HowTo/Service). LLMs may use this to extract concise answers, but should verify details against the visible page content.

## Safety & disclaimers
- Health information on HDmall is educational and not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Encourage users to seek care from licensed professionals for personal medical concerns.
